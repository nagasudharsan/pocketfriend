Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how to make the most of Pocket Friend

👛 What is Pocket Friend?

Pocket Friend is a student pocket-money manager designed for phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. It lets you record allowances from parents, track daily canteen food and travel expenses, set monthly category budgets, and save up for your goals.

💰 How do I add pocket money?

Click "+ Add Transaction" in the sidebar (or tap the center ➕ button on mobile), choose Add Pocket Money, enter the amount, select the source (Allowance, Parents, Gift, Scholarship), and save!

🔒 Is my student data safe & private?

Yes! All records are stored securely in your browser's private isolated storage. No bank account access, PINs, or passwords to real financial services are ever requested, ensuring complete privacy.

📲 How do I install this as an App on Android or PC?

Pocket Friend is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). In Chrome or Edge, click the Install App button in the address bar (or "Add to Home screen" in mobile browser menu) to run it as a standalone app!
